Last Seen In

Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

This policy covers the "Last Seen In uploader" app, which the owner of the Last Seen In channel uses to publish the channel's own videos to its own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information of the channel's own account (user.info.basic) and permission to publish videos to it (video.publish).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the channel's own account (youtube.upload).

The only account that signs in to the app is the channel owner's. The app does not access, collect or store information about viewers or any other users.

How information is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the channel owner's private storage and are used only to publish the channel's videos. They are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app uses only the youtube.upload permission. It does not read, store or share any YouTube data, including other channels' data, comments, analytics or subscriber information, and it places no cookies or tracking on any device.

Deleting access and data

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google security settings page (and from TikTok's app permissions settings), which immediately stops all publishing. Stored sign-in tokens are deleted within 7 days of revocation or on request to the contact address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or data deletion requests: lastseeninyt@gmail.com.
